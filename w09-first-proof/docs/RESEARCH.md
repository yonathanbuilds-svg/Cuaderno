# Research sources and limits

Accessed October 6, 2026 (Mexico City). Official sources only. No vendor effectiveness claims are treated as independent evidence.

1. Forage, 'Forage for Education - How It Works'. https://education.theforage.com/education-how-it-works
   Official description of free employer-created learning simulations. Evidence for an existing global format, not proof that a small Mexican retailer will recognize our artifact. Local adaptation is a hypothesis; do not claim Forage is absent from Mexico.
2. TestGorilla, 'AI interviews'. https://support.testgorilla.com/hc/en-us/articles/41160350842523-AI-interviews
   Official documentation distinguishes structured interview answers and skills testing, with reviewable criteria and human score overrides. Claims about fairness or rigor are the vendor's.
3. TestGorilla, 'Introducing Job Simulations'. https://support.testgorilla.com/hc/en-us/articles/46448569600411-Introducing-Job-Simulations
   Official description of roleplay, replayable responses and criterion scoring. Its documented launch library does not permit custom simulations; that limits task localization. Availability and product scope can change.
4. Gmyrek, Winkler and Garganta, ILO/World Bank Working Paper 121, July 2024, 'Buffer or Bottleneck? Employment Exposure to Generative AI and the Digital Divide in Latin America'. https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2024-07/WP121_web.pdf
   Models exposure and infrastructure constraints; this is not a measured count of AI-caused layoffs, a 2026 Mexican census, or a predictor of an individual person's future.
