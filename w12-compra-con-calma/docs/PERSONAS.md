# Customer Personas v1
Week 12 | Formal design hypotheses, updated when research corrects them

## Persona 1 - Buyer A (private reference case)
Source: A real buyer case reported by Yonathan concerns a sofa purchase and uncertainty about the total cost. Identity, age and purchase amount are withheld from this public research note.
- Context: considering that sofa; retailer, city, charges, budget and payment mode are unknown.
- Need, paraphrased from Yonathan: understand what the sofa will cost in total before deciding. Not a direct quotation from Buyer A.
- What he does today: not established; ask how he gathers and compares charges.
- What he would pay: unknown; ask after a useful review, without suggesting a price first.
- What would make him quit: hypothesis - forced registration before any value, a misleading total or too many fields.
- Task: enter base price, mark unknown delivery, obtain the quote, update the review and compare with his own limit.

## Persona 2 - Mariana, 32
Synthetic customer for interface testing, not evidence of market demand.
- Context: Spanish-speaking furniture shopper, comfortable with simple mobile forms, wants an inspectable total.
- Need in the persona's words: "Quiero saber cuánto terminaría pagando y qué tengo que preguntar." This is authored persona dialogue.
- What she does today: hypothesis - compares the product page with messages from the store and a calculator.
- What she would pay: unknown; no invented willingness-to-pay figure.
- What would make her quit: a blank charge becoming zero, a total that looks final despite missing costs, or a hidden save action.
- Task: review an invented MXN 8,900 sofa, unknown delivery and MXN 500 assembly against an invented MXN 10,000 limit.

## Research plan
Ask Buyer A to walk through his actual quote, record charges and baseline time with permission, then repeat with the tool. Ask what changed his decision, what remained unclear and whether he would pay. Use invented data for all public demonstrations. Recruit four further consenting buyers for Week 13. Do not report planned interviews as completed.
